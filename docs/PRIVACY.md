# Claimr — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 7 August 2026

Claimr is a game you play by walking real streets. That means it handles
location data, and this page says exactly what it collects, why, who else sees
it, and how to delete it.

If anything here doesn't match what the app does, the app is wrong and we want
to know: **munjpranay0@gmail.com**

---

## The short version

- We record your location **only while a run is active**, and we use it to draw
  your trail and work out the territory you enclosed.
- **We never show your live location to another player.** Other players see
  claimed territory on a map. They do not see where you are.
- You can delete everything we hold, from inside the app, at any time.
- We do not sell your data, and there are no advertising trackers in Claimr.

---

## What we collect

### Location

Collected **during an active run only** — when you have pressed Start Run and
the run has not ended.

Tracking continues while the app is in the background or your screen is locked,
because a game you can only play with the phone held in front of your face would
be unsafe. iOS shows the blue status-bar indicator throughout, and Android shows
a persistent notification, so it is always visible when tracking is on.

From your location we store:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The polygon you enclosed | It is your territory. It is the game. |
| Distance and duration of the run | Stats, rewards, and anti-cheat |
| Your best route ("ghost run") | So you can race your own past run |
| An approximate home base, if you set one | Streaks and local leaderboards. Stored at **block level**, never as an address. |

We do **not** keep a continuous history of everywhere you walked. Your live
trail exists on your phone during the run; what reaches our server is the
finished shape and the run's totals.

### Account

Claimr signs you in **anonymously** by default — no email, no password, no
phone number. We generate a random player ID. If you set a display name,
colour, or avatar, we store those.

### Photos

Only if you use photo flags. Then the photo you choose is uploaded and attached
to a map location you picked. Nothing else in your camera roll is read.

### Diagnostics

To find bugs and keep the game fair, we log events such as: app opened, run
started, run finished, capture succeeded or rejected, trail expired, battery
used during a run, and crash messages. These are tied to your player ID.

### What we never collect

No contacts, no browsing activity, no advertising identifiers, no microphone,
no data from other apps, and no location outside an active run.

## Who else sees it

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Everything described above | Our database and authentication |
| **Mapbox** | Map requests, which reveal roughly where you are looking | Drawing the map |

Both are processors acting for us. Neither is given your data to sell.

Other **players** see: your display name, colour, avatar, total area, and the
territory you own. They never see your live position, your route, your home
base, or your account details.

---

## Deleting your data

In the app: **Profile → Delete my data**.

This removes your player record, your territory, your runs, your ghost route
and your diagnostic events, and signs you out. It is immediate and it cannot be
undone. You do not have to email anyone to make it happen.

Backups are retained for up to 30 days for disaster recovery, after which
deleted data is gone from those too.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR in the UK/EU, CCPA in California, and similar
laws elsewhere) you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your
data, and to object to processing. The in-app delete covers the most common
request instantly. For anything else, email **munjpranay0@gmail.com** and we
will respond within 30 days.

Our lawful basis for processing location is **performance of a contract** —
without it there is no game to play.

---

## Children

Claimr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data. A game that sends people walking to real places is not something we
want young children using unsupervised. If you believe a child has given us
data, email us and we will delete it.

---

## Safety

Claimr asks you to walk in the real world. Keep your eyes up, obey traffic
signals, and do not enter private property or unsafe areas to claim territory.
Runs auto-pause at vehicle speed. You are responsible for your own safety and
for following local laws.
