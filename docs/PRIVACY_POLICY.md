# Claimr — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-23_

Claimr ("we", "us") is a location-based game. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and your rights. **Plain-language summary: we use your location
while you play to draw your trail and claim turf. We show the land you claim to
other players — never your live location. You can delete all your data anytime
from the Profile screen.**

## Information we collect

- **Location data (while the app is in use):** Your GPS position and speed while
  a run is active, to draw your trail, detect closed loops, and record claimed
  turf. We collect location only during active use, not in the background.
- **Account data:** An anonymous account identifier (you can play without an
  email), a display name, and a chosen color.
- **Gameplay data:** The polygons (turf) you claim, run statistics (distance,
  area, duration), and your total area held.

We do **not** collect your name, email (unless you later add one), contacts,
photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell your data.

## How we use it

- To provide the game: draw trails, capture turf, show the shared map,
  compute the leaderboard.
- To keep play fair and safe: server-side speed checks (anti-cheat) and
  auto-pausing runs at vehicle speed.

## What other players can see

Other players see the **turf you claim** (as colored areas on the shared map)
and your display name and total area on the leaderboard. **Other players never
see your live or precise location.** Any "home base" feature is shown only at
approximate, block-level precision — never your exact address.

## Data sharing & storage

- Data is stored on our backend provider, **Supabase** (PostgreSQL), secured
  with row-level security so only you can modify your own data.
- We share data only as needed to run the service (our hosting provider). We do
  not sell personal data.

## Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

- **Delete your data:** Profile → "Delete my data" permanently erases your
  account, turf, and run history. You may also email us to request deletion.
- **Access/portability:** Contact us to request a copy of your data.

## Children

Claimr is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data
from children under 13.
